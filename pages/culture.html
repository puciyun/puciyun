Our Story

Established in Pengzhou, Sichuan Province, Sichuan Puciyun Technology Co., Ltd was founded with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and enterprise needs in the global market. Starting from a team of passionate engineers and technology enthusiasts, we have grown into a comprehensive technology solutions provider serving enterprises across multiple continents.

Our journey began with a focus on cloud computing solutions, helping enterprises transition from legacy systems to modern, scalable infrastructure. As the technology landscape evolved, we expanded our expertise to include artificial intelligence, machine learning, and advanced data analytics. Today, we proudly serve over 500 enterprise clients worldwide, providing comprehensive solutions that drive digital transformation.

What started as a local initiative has become a global operation. Our team of experienced professionals, combined with our commitment to continuous innovation and customer success, has established Puciyun Technology as a trusted partner for enterprises seeking reliable, cutting-edge technology solutions.

How We Work

Talent Development

We invest in professional growth through structured technical tracks, leadership pathways, and practical learning communities. Team members are encouraged to rotate across projects, mentor peers, and contribute to architecture standards.

Our development framework includes engineering guilds, quarterly skill reviews, and sponsored certifications in cloud platforms, security, and data technologies. This helps individuals build depth while maintaining a broad product perspective.

We also prioritize healthy work practices by promoting realistic planning, focused execution windows, and cross-team support mechanisms. Strong outcomes come from sustainable teams, not short-term intensity.
